Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Wakeo is an alarm app made by SBS Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Using Wakeo as a guest

You can use Wakeo without an account. Your alarms, settings and wake-up history then stay on your device and are not sent to us.

If you create an account (optional)

You can sign in with email, Google or Apple so your streak follows you to a new phone. When you do, we store:

  • Your email address and, if you provide it, your name. (If you choose Sign in with Apple, you can hide your email.)
  • Your alarms, your daily wake-up records (the time you woke and the mission you completed), your bedtime schedule and your app settings, so they can sync between your devices.
  • Your subscription status: whether Wakeo Premium is active and when it ends, and an identifier Apple gives each subscription so we can tell when it renews, lapses or is refunded.

This information is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and is protected so each account can only read its own rows.

Purchases

Wakeo Premium is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Apple sends us signed confirmation of your purchase, which we verify to unlock Premium.

Camera, microphone and speech

  • Camera. Missions such as push-ups, squats and photo missions use the camera to check that you did the task. This analysis happens on your device, and the camera feed is not recorded or uploaded.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Missions where you read a verse or say a phrase use speech recognition to check what you said. Apple provides speech recognition, and it may process your audio on Apple’s servers under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not record or store your audio.
  • Notifications. Used only so your alarm can reach you. They are created on your device.

Optional features that send photos

Both of these are off by default, require an account, and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

  • Check photos with AI. If you turn this on, a mission photo is sent to our server, which passes it to an AI service (Anthropic) solely to judge whether the photo shows what the mission asked for. We do not keep the photo after the check.
  • Contribute mission photos. If you turn this on, your mission photos are uploaded to us and used to improve how Wakeo verifies missions. Turning it off stops future uploads and discards photos that have not yet been sent.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app, and they process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — account, database and file hosting.
  • Apple — App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple and speech recognition.
  • Google — only if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • Anthropic — only if you turn on “Check photos with AI”.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your data

Guest data lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app. For an account, you can delete it any time in Settings → Delete account. That permanently removes your account and the data tied to it, including any photos you contributed. You can also email us at wakeoteam@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your data or for deletion.

Children

Wakeo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access to accounts is restricted. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.

Contact
