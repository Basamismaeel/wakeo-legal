My alarm did not ring

  • Allow notifications for Wakeo: Settings → Wakeo → Notifications (and check Settings → Notifications → Wakeo).
  • Turn the volume up, and check that a Focus mode is not silencing Wakeo.
  • Keep your phone charged; very low battery or Low Power Mode can interfere with alarms.
  • Make sure the alarm is switched on and set for the days you expect.

A mission will not complete

  • Allow Camera, Microphone and Speech Recognition for Wakeo in iOS Settings → Wakeo.
  • For push-ups and squats, prop your phone up so the camera can see your whole body, in good light.
  • For voice missions, speak clearly in a quiet room.

Restore or manage Premium

  • Restore: sign in to the same Wakeo account, then open Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wakeo.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete my account and data

Open Wakeo → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its data. Or email us and we will do it for you.
